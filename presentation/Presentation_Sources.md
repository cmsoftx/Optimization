## Assumptions and caveats stated in the deck

* **Simulated business parameters.** Costs, capacities and fleet are assumptions from `config.yaml`, not dataset values. Only relative comparisons are meaningful.
* **Approximate distances.** All distances are great-circle × 1.2 circuity. They are not road-network distances, and route lines are drawn as straight segments.
* **Demand points.** Demand sits at customer coordinates, because the destination fields are not geographically usable.
* **Sequential optimisation.** The CFLP is optimised first, then routing. Their objectives are not optimised jointly.
* **Time-limited solves.** These are reported as incumbents with their gaps, never as optimal.
* **One Puerto Rico Experiment C plan.** CBC reported `optimal`, but the lower bound Pyomo parsed was inconsistent. That bound is reported as unavailable (see report §17).
* **Dashboard screenshots.** OpenStreetMap tiles were blocked in the build environment, so the maps show markers without a basemap.
* **Title-slide fields.** Student name, university, department and supervisor are editable placeholders.

## Rounding

* Percentages on the slides are rounded to one decimal place, and costs to whole dollars.
* The Random baseline mean ($30,118) can vary by a few cents between runs. Its 30 allocation subproblems are solved to CBC's 1e-4 relative tolerance, so alternative near-optimal allocations are possible.
